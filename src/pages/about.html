Who We Are

Experience built since 2004.

Evroskop was founded in 2004 and has grown through dedication to our work, commitment to product quality, and a practical engineering approach to industrial projects.

We provide the design and implementation of industrial projects according to applicable standards and environmental requirements, either as a complete turnkey solution or through equipment supply according to the customer's needs.

Our work brings together equipment, automation, mechanical design, programming, integration, commissioning, tooling, and technical support into complete production solutions.

Our Approach

Start with the production requirement.

We believe the right industrial solution begins with understanding the process rather than simply selecting a machine or robot.

Material, production volume, cycle time, workpiece geometry, quality requirements, available space, operator interaction, and future production needs all influence the final solution.

1

Understand

Analyze the application, process, workpiece, production targets, and constraints.

2

Engineer

Develop the technical concept, select equipment, and design the required tooling and integration.

3

Implement

Install, integrate, program, commission, test, and optimize the production solution.

4

Support

Continue supporting the customer after the system enters production.
